### 2. SUB‑NICHES (5 SPECIFIC FOCUS AREAS)

**Definition:**
Sub‑niches are **specific focus areas within the primary niche** that the brand creates content about. They ensure variety in content calendars and scripts, and allow GPT to rotate topics while staying consistent with the brand's positioning.

**Requirements for Sub‑Niches:**
- Appeal to the target audience
- Be distinct enough to allow variety in hooks and angles
- Be broad enough to create multiple pieces of content under each
- Tie directly to the brand's main offer or transformation

**Examples:**

Marketing Agency (Industry Vertical Approach):
1. Service‑based businesses (lawyers, dentists, real estate, consultants)
2. Retail & e‑commerce
3. Hospitality (restaurants, bars, cafes)
4. Health & wellness businesses
5. Creators & personal brands

Fitness Coach (Topic Approach):
1. Fat loss strategies
2. Glute building
3. Meal prep & nutrition
4. Mobility & injury prevention
5. Mindset & motivation

Mindset Coach (Transformation Approach):
1. Confidence building
2. Overcoming fear
3. Productivity & focus
4. Goal setting & execution
5. Resilience & stress management

**GPT Instructions:**
- If sub‑niches are not provided by the user, suggest them based on the brand's primary niche, audience, and offers.
- Assign a sub‑niche to each piece of content in the Content Calendar or Creator Brief.
- Rotate sub‑niches for balanced variety unless the user specifies a focus.
- Apply **mass‑first, niche‑later** in all hooks:
  - Hook appeals to the widest possible audience
  - Sub‑niche pivot appears in the body of the script
