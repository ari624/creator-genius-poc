# Proven Blueprint Structures

## 1. LISTICLE FORMAT
**Hook:** "The [number] [things] that will [benefit]"

**Structure:**
1. Intro
2. Item 1 (Name it → Explain it → Why it matters)
3. Item 2
4. Item 3
5. ...
6. Recap
7. CTA

**Works for:** Educational, value-only content

**Example:** "5 Tennis Tricks You Need", "3 Mistakes Killing Your Growth"

---

## 2. PROBLEM-SOLUTION FORMAT
**Hook:** "If you're struggling with [problem], watch this"

**Structure:**
1. Identify problem (relatable)
2. Consequences (what happens if ignored)
3. Solution (the answer)
4. How to implement (actionable steps)
5. CTA

**Works for:** Relatable pain points, product pitches

**Example:** "If You're Struggling With Fat Loss", "Can't Build Muscle? Here's Why"

---

## 3. TRANSFORMATION FORMAT
**Hook:** "How I went from [before] to [after]"

**Structure:**
1. Starting point (where you were)
2. Obstacles (challenges faced)
3. Discovery (the breakthrough)
4. Action (what you did)
5. Results (proof)
6. CTA

**Works for:** Personal stories, testimonials, case studies

**Example:** "How I Went From 200lbs to Shredded", "From Broke to 6 Figures"

---

## 4. MISTAKE FORMAT
**Hook:** "You're making this mistake with [topic]"

**Structure:**
1. Common approach (what most people do)
2. Why it's wrong (the problem)
3. What to do instead (the solution)
4. Results you'll see (the benefit)
5. CTA

**Works for:** Contrarian takes, correcting misconceptions

**Example:** "Stop Doing Cardio This Way", "You're Training Your Glutes Wrong"

---

## 5. TUTORIAL FORMAT
**Hook:** "Here's how to [achieve result] in [timeframe]"

**Structure:**
1. Overview (what you'll learn)
2. Step 1 (detailed)
3. Step 2 (detailed)
4. Step 3 (detailed)
5. Common mistakes to avoid
6. CTA

**Works for:** How-to, educational, skill-building content

**Example:** "How to Build Muscle in 30 Days", "Master This Tennis Serve in 10 Minutes"

---

## 6. COMPARISON FORMAT
**Hook:** "[Option A] vs [Option B]: Which is better?"

**Structure:**
1. Intro both options
2. Option A pros/cons
3. Option B pros/cons
4. Verdict (which is better and why)
5. CTA

**Works for:** Product reviews, strategy comparisons

**Example:** "Free Weights vs Machines", "Keto vs Intermittent Fasting"

---

## 7. MYTH-BUSTING FORMAT
**Hook:** "Everything you know about [topic] is wrong"

**Structure:**
1. Common belief (the myth)
2. Why it's a myth (debunk it)
3. The truth (what's actually true)
4. Evidence (proof/research)
5. CTA

**Works for:** Contrarian content, educational

**Example:** "Carbs Don't Make You Fat", "You Don't Need 8 Hours of Sleep"
